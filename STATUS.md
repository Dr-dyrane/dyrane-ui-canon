## Current hypotheses

These are not final canon rules.

1. **Identity precedes content.** The Icon Orb is a governed semantic identity primitive, not a decorative leading icon.
2. **Color expresses signal, not category decoration.** Dyrane color tends to communicate confidence, attention, intervention, progress, or role.
3. **Structure precedes decoration.** Hierarchy should emerge from topology, spacing, typography, tone, and progressive disclosure before borders.
4. **Motion explains.** Animation is valid when it communicates topology, causality, state, focus, or continuity.
5. **Responsive design transforms presentation.** Mobile is not compressed desktop.
6. **Intelligence remains accountable.** AI guidance must preserve evidence, confidence, reversibility, and user agency.
7. **Complexity belongs behind calm surfaces.** Rich system behavior should not require visibly busy interfaces.

## Known risks

- Overfitting the canon to My Finance.
- Confusing recent preferences with durable portfolio-wide principles.
- Treating framework defaults as authored Dyrane decisions.
- Promoting attractive one-off treatments without cross-project evidence.
- Documenting Apple conventions without adapting them to web, PWA, and React Native realities.
- Creating too much research material without a compact build-facing reference layer.
- Allowing Icon Orb or semantic color to become mandatory where another identity mechanism is more appropriate.
